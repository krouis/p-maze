# Privacy Policy

**Pixel Maze (p-maze)** is built to be a safe, clean, and private game for children.

## Key Privacy Practices

### 1. No Personal Data Collection

The game does not request, collect, or store any personal data. There are no registration forms, profile creators, name requests, or login systems.

### 2. No Tracking or Analytics

We do not use any tracking scripts, third-party analytics tools (like Google Analytics), or event monitors. We do not track how children play the game.

### 3. No Advertisements

There are absolutely no ads, banners, video popups, or promotions inside the game interface.

### 4. No Social Sharing or Chat

There are no multiplayer features, chat systems, or social media integration hooks, eliminating any risk of contact with strangers.

### 5. No Cookies

The game does not use any browser cookies. No cookie consent banners are displayed because no cookies exist.

---

## Local Data Storage

All game progression data (completed levels, settings, and player coordinates) is saved **strictly locally** on your device using the browser's `localStorage` API.

- **Device Isolation**: Your progress is stored locally inside the browser. It is never uploaded to any backend server.
- **Data Removal**: Clearing your browser site data or cookies/cache will permanently remove your progress.
- **No Transfers**: Because progress is purely offline, you cannot transfer your unlocked levels to another phone or computer.
